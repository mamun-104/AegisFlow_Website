The current problem

Employees are already using AI tools to write, analyse and automate work. The risk is that client data, intellectual property and confidential business information can be pasted into public AI systems without enough control.

Data leakage

PII, financial details, client records, code and internal strategy may be shared in prompts or uploaded files.

Adversarial risks

Prompt injection and unsafe instructions can manipulate AI workflows or expose confidential information.

Compliance pressure

SMEs need evidence that AI use is controlled, especially when working with regulated clients and supply chains.

How AegisFlow aims to solve it

AegisFlow intercepts prompts at the point of interaction, detects risky content, redacts sensitive information and creates an audit trail before the prompt reaches external AI systems.

Inspect

Detects PII, IP, confidential data and unsafe prompt behaviour.

Sanitise

Masks or removes risky information before it leaves the organisation.

Govern

Applies policies and records evidence for audit and compliance.
